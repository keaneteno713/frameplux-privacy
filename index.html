简体中文 · English

拍摄与权限

Frame+ 在设备上处理拍摄画面、声音、滤镜和边框。应用无需账号，无广告跟踪、云同步或内购。应用不会主动向开发者上传照片、视频、录音或导入的素材。

  • 相机权限用于取景、拍照和录像。
  • 麦克风权限用于录制视频及受支持的 Live 拍摄声音。
  • 照片图库仅添加权限用于保存成品。导入图片或文件时，应用通过系统选择器接收你主动选中的项目。

你可以在 iPhone 的「设置」中调整权限。关闭权限会影响相应功能。

设备上的数据

设置、导入素材和拍摄处理所需的文件保存在设备上。尚未保存到「照片」的作品可能留在应用中供重试。应用拍摄的照片可包含拍摄时间、曝光和设备型号等拍摄信息。应用不请求定位权限。

已保存的作品可在系统「照片」中管理。iCloud 照片或设备备份依你的 Apple 系统设置运行。

TestFlight 与反馈

Apple 通过 TestFlight 分发测试版本。根据邀请和反馈方式，开发者可能收到 TestFlight 提供的安装和使用信息、故障诊断，以及你选择提交的联系方式、反馈或截图。开发者用这些信息定位问题和改进应用。也可以使用不含个人信息的测试场景反馈。

开发者只保留排查问题所需的反馈、截图、录屏和联系方式，并在问题关闭后 30 天内删除自己保存的副本，包括下载到本地的文件和邮箱中的相关邮件及附件。

联系与删除

如需咨询隐私问题或请求删除开发者持有的反馈、附件和联系信息，请邮件联系 frameplux@gmail.com。已保存的作品可通过「照片」管理，Apple 独立持有的数据可通过 Apple 提供的隐私方式管理。

Frame+ Privacy Policy

Capture and permissions

Frame+ processes captured images, sound, looks, and frames on your device. The app requires no account and has no advertising tracking, cloud sync, or in-app purchases. It does not automatically upload your photos, videos, recordings, or imports to the developer.

  • Camera access provides a viewfinder and enables photo and video capture.
  • Microphone access records sound for video and supported Live Photo features.
  • Add-only Photos access saves finished captures. When importing images or files, the app receives the items you select in the system picker.

You can change permissions in iPhone Settings. Turning a permission off limits the corresponding features.

On-device data

Preferences, imports, and files used during capture and processing are stored on your device. Items waiting to be saved to Photos may remain in the app for retry. Photos captured in the app can include capture details such as time, exposure, and device model. The app does not request location access.

You can manage saved captures in Photos. iCloud Photos and device backup are governed by your Apple system settings.

TestFlight and feedback

Apple distributes beta builds through TestFlight. Depending on how you join a beta or send feedback, the developer may receive TestFlight installation and usage information, diagnostics, or contact information, feedback, and screenshots you choose to share. The developer uses this information to investigate issues and improve the app. You can use test scenes that contain no personal information.

The developer keeps only the feedback, screenshots, screen recordings, and contact information needed to investigate an issue. The developer deletes copies it has saved within 30 days after the issue is closed, including downloaded files and related emails and attachments in the developer’s mailbox.

Contact and deletion

For privacy questions or to request deletion of feedback, attachments, or contact information held by the developer, email frameplux@gmail.com. You can manage saved captures in Photos. Use Apple’s privacy controls for data Apple holds independently.
